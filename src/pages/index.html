Shoot Vision

Guide the cull.

Add references or describe the look, then confirm what belongs in this shoot. Make room for deliberate blur, grain and closed-eye candids. Save it as a preset, or skip for Classic.

How Vision works

Use up to 50 references, words, or both. Confirmed intentions can relax or reorder decisions, but never create a new Reject. Your target count still applies.

References help rank content and look. Resemblance alone cannot put a weaker frame ahead of a sharp, open-eyed face until your own picks teach that preference.

As you review, RedWax may suggest a change to your Vision. You decide whether to allow it. Blank frames, unreadable files and unrecoverable exposure remain rejected.

Questions and answers.

Which cameras does RedWax support?

The RAW formats macOS reads: Canon CR2 and CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Fujifilm RAF, Olympus ORF, Panasonic RW2 and DNG, plus JPEG and HEIC. RedWax culls from each file's embedded preview, so it works even when a camera is newer than macOS.

Will RedWax change my photos?

Never. Originals are only opened for reading. Ratings go into sidecar files, merged with what's there and backed up first, and moving rejects into a folder can always be undone.

My shoot is already in Lightroom. Can I still use it?

Yes. Export the sidecars, then choose Metadata › Read Metadata from Files in Lightroom Classic. That replaces ratings set in the catalog, so RedWax checks with you first.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Analysis, learning and export all run on your Mac. Only subscriptions go through the Mac App Store.

How does it learn my taste?

Start with shoots you’ve already rated. During a cull, learning stays in that shoot; at export, choose what teaches a profile. RedWax learns most from your changes and confirmations, less from frames you reviewed without changing, and nothing from unseen frames. Keep up to five profiles, and undo any shoot’s lesson later.

What if blur or closed eyes are part of the photo?

Add an optional Shoot Vision using references, words or a preset. Only intentions you confirm affect the cull. Vision can relax or reorder calls, but never create a new Reject. Blank frames, unreadable files and exposure beyond recovery still cannot be kept. The strongest allowed frames compete for your target count.

Can I change the Vision while I review?

Yes. Open the Vision pill below the shoot title. After five or more keeps or rejects that suggest a different intention, RedWax may offer a change. It always asks, including when you start in Classic.

Which Macs can run it?

Any Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
