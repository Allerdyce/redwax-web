Getting started

RedWax works straight from the card or from a copy on your disk, and never changes the originals.

  1. 1Open a shootDrag the folder onto RedWax, or press ⌘O. Thumbnails appear within seconds.
  2. 2Add a direction, if you have oneDrop references or describe the look, then confirm the intentions you want. Save a preset, or skip for Classic. Analysis runs while you choose.
  3. 3ReviewWalk the moments, change any call with P, X or U, and set the target with the slider in the cull bar.
  4. 4ExportPress ⌘E for sidecars, Copy selects or Move rejects. Review what this shoot could teach, then teach this profile, another, or Skip. Moving rejects can be undone.

New to RedWax? On the Welcome screen, choose Try it on sample photos.

Lightroom Classic and Capture One

Lightroom Classic

Export with sidecars, then import the folder: your stars and colour labels come with it. If the shoot is already in your catalog, select its photos and choose Metadata › Read Metadata from Files. That replaces ratings and labels set in the catalog, so RedWax asks before it writes.

Capture One

Choose the Capture One preset in the export sheet. It writes star ratings and colour labels with English names. In Capture One, set Auto Sync Sidecar XMP to Load or Full Sync. Capture One doesn't read picks.

RedWax never writes into a catalog or into your original files.

Ratings and taste profiles

Your rating convention

Tell RedWax what your stars mean in Settings, for example 1 star for a keeper and 3 for a hero, and it writes the same way.

Taste profiles

Choose New Profile and add rated shoots with their sidecars. In a new cull, learning stays in that shoot until you choose to teach a profile at export. Changes and confirmations count most, reviewed frames count less, and unseen frames teach nothing. Keep up to five profiles.

Moving to a new Mac

Export a profile as a file, then import it on the new Mac. Your taste comes with you.

Reference images and presets

Use up to 50 reference images from files or drag them from a browser. References help rank both content and look. Confirm the suggested intentions, save a Vision as a preset, and reopen it from the shoot header. RedWax asks before changing intentions as you review.

Troubleshooting

“This folder is read-only.”

Copy the shoot to a writable drive, or choose Copy selects instead.

My camera is newer than macOS.

RedWax culls from each file's embedded preview, so it works before macOS can decode the RAW.

Ratings don't appear in Lightroom.

The shoot was imported before you exported. Select its photos and choose Metadata › Read Metadata from Files.

Labels don't appear in Capture One.

Export with the Capture One preset, and set Auto Sync Sidecar XMP to Load or Full Sync.

Where did my rejects go?

Into the _RedWax Rejected folder beside the shoot. RedWax can move them back at any time.

Why did RedWax keep a blurred photo?

A confirmed intention may allow deliberate motion blur to compete for a place in the cull. The reason says when Vision affected a call. Open the Vision pill to change it, or choose Classic. Vision never adds a Reject.

How do I undo what a shoot taught my profile?

Open Taste Profiles in the sidebar, choose the profile, and in its history click Undo what this shoot taught beside the shoot. Only that shoot’s lesson is removed.
