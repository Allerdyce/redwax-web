Privacy policy Your photos stay on your Mac. Photo analysis, references and taste profiles stay on your Mac. RedWax has no accounts or analytics. Purchases go through Apple; support receives only what you choose to send. EvidencePair LLC · Last updated [DATE] Draft for review

Stays on your Mac Every photo is analysed on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, ever. No account Nothing to sign up for. Your subscription lives with the App Store. Data Not Collected That's our App Store privacy label. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

On this page Your photos What RedWax keeps on your Mac Faces Reference images Your files Purchases Support email and diagnostics Changes to this policy Contact Your photos RedWax reads your photos to sort them, and all of that work happens on your Mac. The part of the app that analyses photos has no network access at all. Your photos, and anything RedWax learns from them, are never sent to us or to anyone else. What RedWax keeps on your Mac To be fast the second time, RedWax keeps a small library in its own sandboxed folder on your Mac, which other apps can't read: the shoots you've opened, and permission to reopen their folders camera details from each photo, such as the time it was taken and the lens measurements such as sharpness and exposure, and the decisions you've made your taste profiles and settings thumbnails, capped at 5 GB, with the oldest cleared first None of it leaves your Mac unless you export a taste profile yourself to move it to another Mac. Faces RedWax finds faces and eyes so it can tell whether a face is sharp and whether eyes are open. It never works out who anyone is, and it keeps no record that could identify a person. What it does keep, measurements like eye openness, stays on your Mac with the rest of the library. Reference images Reference images, descriptions, confirmed intentions and saved Vision presets stay on your Mac. References help RedWax understand and rank the look and content you want, never identify anyone. Reference images are deleted when you delete their Vision. Your files RedWax opens only the folders you choose. It never writes to or deletes your original photos. When you export, it writes ratings and labels into sidecar files, backing up any sidecar it changes first, and it moves rejects only into a folder beside the shoot that it can move back. Purchases Trials and subscriptions go through the App Store. Apple handles payment and checks your subscription, and we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.
